About

Building systems teams can operate with confidence

I’m a Platform and Infrastructure Engineer with 6+ years of hands-on experience across cloud, DevOps, production reliability and the systems behind dependable software.

Professional focus

I work where software meets infrastructure

My background began in software delivery and expanded through AWS, Kubernetes, CI/CD and observability into sustained ownership of Linux-based production platforms. That progression helps me follow a problem across application, infrastructure, database, network and edge boundaries instead of treating each layer in isolation.

I’m strongest when a system is difficult to understand, a change carries production risk, or repeated manual work needs to become a reliable operating process. I investigate the current state, make the smallest useful change, validate what happened and leave behind automation, monitoring or documentation that makes the next decision easier.

Builder, operator, mentor and technical instructor.

Ownership

How I operate

Ownership means understanding the system before changing it, protecting production while work is in progress and making the result clear to the people who depend on it.

See the approach in case studies →
  1. 01Investigate symptoms, dependencies and failure paths before choosing a fix.
  2. 02Prefer controlled changes with explicit validation and rollback conditions.
  3. 03Turn repeated recovery or review work into automation, monitoring and runbooks.
  4. 04Communicate technical risk in terms engineering, product and operational teams can use.
