About

How I think about systems and responsibility

I’m a Platform and Infrastructure Engineer who approaches production systems as both a builder and an operator.

My path developed through software, cloud and DevOps work into sustained responsibility for production infrastructure, with an emphasis on Linux, databases, observability, automation and reversible change.

I work across technical boundaries, turning diagnosis into careful action and communicating risk clearly when changes affect applications, infrastructure, data or real-time services.

Teaching Cloud, DevOps and cybersecurity extends the same practice by translating complex systems into applied instruction and practical laboratories.

Operating principles

  1. 01 Understand the system before changing it.
  2. 02 Make validation and rollback part of the design.
  3. 03 Communicate risk in terms other teams can use.
  4. 04 Teach the reasoning, not only the procedure.
