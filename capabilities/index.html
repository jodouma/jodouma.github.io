Operating method

Observe the system, isolate the risk, preserve the way back.

  1. Understand: map the request path, dependencies and ownership boundary.
  2. Classify: separate symptoms from the constrained or hostile layer.
  3. Control: make the narrowest safe, observable and reversible change.
  4. Validate: test customer paths, dependencies, failure modes and recovery.
  5. Institutionalize: add alerts, runbooks, reset paths and team knowledge.
