My journey

From software foundations to production platform ownership

My career grew from software delivery into cloud and DevOps engineering, then sustained ownership of production platforms and applied technical teaching.

The path was practical rather than perfectly linear. Each stage added a new layer—software workflows, cloud delivery, Kubernetes and observability, production operations, incident leadership, and finally the responsibility of teaching those systems to others.

Professional evolution

Increasing scope, deeper operating responsibility

  1. 01

    January–July 2019

    Software foundations

    Building systems around real business workflows

    At D.E.S, I worked on business-management and CRM workflows, data migration, automation, dashboards and deployment support. It was an early lesson that software is valuable when it fits the way people actually work.

    Review the full experience →
  2. 02

    January 2020–February 2021

    Cloud and Kubernetes

    Moving from application delivery into platform architecture

    At iPrécision, my scope expanded across AWS, Amazon EKS, Kubernetes, Helm, Jenkins delivery workflows and centralized Elastic/OpenSearch telemetry. A controlled load test connected anomaly detection to operational investigation without treating detection as automatic mitigation.

    Read the AWS EKS and observability case study →
  3. 03

    March 2021–June 2022

    Cloud delivery

    Deepening AWS, automation and observability work

    At UBIAI, I supported AWS workloads across ECS, EC2 and Lambda, improved CI/CD and observability, and investigated memory and scalability questions through metrics rather than assumptions.

    Review the role →
  4. 04

    July 2022–July 2026

    Production ownership

    Owning platform reliability across system boundaries

    At Apprentus, I operated the Linux, Nginx, Passenger, Rails, PostgreSQL, Redis, edge, monitoring and real-time systems behind 12 production domains processing more than 80 million monthly requests. The role grew into functional platform leadership, continuous security operations, incident escalation, mentoring and rollback-ready coordination across teams.

    Explore production case studies →Review production scope →
  5. 05

    2025–2026

    Teaching and mentoring

    Turning operating experience into practical learning

    At Leaders University Nabeul, I teach Cloud, DevOps and cybersecurity through applied environments and laboratories. The objective is to help future engineers understand not only how to make a system work, but how to validate, secure and recover it.

    Explore teaching and mentoring →
  6. 06

    Current direction

    Platform and infrastructure

    Building the next chapter around dependable systems

    My focus now is senior Platform, DevOps, SRE, Production Engineering, Cloud Infrastructure and DevSecOps work where practical ownership, careful change and technical depth matter.
