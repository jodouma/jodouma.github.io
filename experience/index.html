Context

  4. Turn repeated diagnosis into documented checks and a more dependable socket reference.

Technical storySecurity controls around production servicesExplore

Context

A 12-domain estate processing more than 80 million monthly requests operated under continuous scanning, probing, scraping, abusive automation and recurring attack attempts.

Challenge

At that scale, security was a traffic-classification problem: protective controls had to distinguish users, trusted crawlers and operational traffic from abuse while preserving availability and origin protection.

Engineering approach

  1. Correlate edge, application and log signals before classifying activity.
  2. Use layered controls across Cloudflare, host protections and application authentication.
  3. Exclude trusted crawler networks before reputation-based enforcement and preserve operator review/reset controls.
  4. Test negative paths and monitor the effect of a rule before widening enforcement.
  5. Preserve rollback and avoid publishing bypass-relevant control details.
