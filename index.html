Security at production scale

Traffic classification was the engineering problem.

At more than 80 million monthly requests, production security meant stopping malicious automation without blocking real users, verified crawlers, internal operations or critical platform flows.

The internet-facing estate operated under continuous automated attacks, scraping, scanning, probing and abusive traffic from changing sources. “Continuous” describes the permanent operating condition; it does not mean that every request was malicious.

Cloudflare, Nginx, AbuseIPDB-informed decisions, trusted exclusions, crawler verification, alerts and reset paths formed a defensive loop that could run repeatedly and reversibly.
