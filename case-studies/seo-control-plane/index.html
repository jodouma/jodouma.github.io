Production engineering case study

Crawler Trust and SEO Visibility Control Plane

I built and operated a crawler trust and visibility control plane across 12 production domains, protecting Google, Bing, OpenAI, Anthropic and other legitimate agents while controlling impersonation, abuse and infrastructure cost.

Context

Across a 12-domain marketplace handling more than 80 million requests per month, discoverability was production infrastructure. Google and Bing brought conventional search visibility; OpenAI, Anthropic and other emerging agents created a newer visibility surface. The same public routes also received impersonators, scrapers and hostile automation using familiar user-agent labels.

Problem

One incorrect block could return 403s to business-critical landing pages and remove them from search; one broad allow rule could grant a forged identity extra access and consume application capacity. I needed one system that separated identity trust from behavior policy, observed the result across all domains and connected investigation to reversible edge action.

Architecture

Crawler and provider registries define expected identity. Incoming observations pass through reverse-DNS and forward-confirmation checks where supported, plus IP/provider trust. Log ingestion joins that result to request behavior, domain, response status and edge/application signals. Dashboards, investigations and alerts expose trusted, suspicious and unresolved paths; Cloudflare workflows apply reviewable allow, rate-control, challenge, restrict or block decisions.

Responsibilities

I architected, implemented and operated the internal FastAPI service, its crawler/provider registries, verification workflow, ingestion, investigation views, dashboards, scheduled jobs, alerts and Cloudflare integration. I also connected technical signals to the business question: are important pages still reachable to the agents that make them discoverable?

Technical approach

  • Classify asserted crawler identities without trusting a user agent by itself.
  • Verify reverse DNS and forward confirmation against provider expectations where supported.
  • Keep identity trust separate from rate, cost and behavior policy.
  • Monitor crawler-specific 403/5xx responses across all production domains.
  • Make trusted, suspicious and unresolved paths visible to operators and business stakeholders.
  • Keep edge decisions controlled, observable, reviewable and reversible.

Validation

Validation covered positive known-provider paths, negative impersonator paths, ambiguous cases held for investigation, crawler-specific 403/5xx monitoring and post-action checks across domains. This mattered in practice: the system had to catch both forged trust and accidental security rules that harmed indexing.

Outcome

Search and AI visibility became an observable platform capability across 12 domains. Legitimate Google, Bing, OpenAI, Anthropic and other access could be protected, while impersonation and abusive behavior remained subject to controlled edge policy. The system balanced discoverability, security and compute cost instead of treating any one of them as absolute.

Technologies

FastAPI, DNS verification, IP trust, Cloudflare workflows, log ingestion, dashboards, alerts and operational automation.
