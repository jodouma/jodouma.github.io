Teaching and mentoring

Practical knowledge for stronger future engineers

I teach to transfer practical knowledge and help future engineers become more capable, confident and ready for the expectations of real technical teams.

October 2024–2026 · Leaders University Nabeul

Technical Instructor — Cloud, DevOps & Cybersecurity

My teaching connects infrastructure concepts to environments students can inspect, operate, break safely and restore. The objective is practical understanding—not a polished demo that only the instructor can run.

Teaching scope

From cloud foundations to applied security

Each area combines technical content with the operating habits that make the work useful beyond the classroom.

Cloud and infrastructure foundations

Virtualisation and Cloud instruction and assessment, with practical Linux, networking, AWS and infrastructure context.

Applied security laboratories

Isolated AWS/Linux and Flask/PostgreSQL environments where learners investigate weaknesses, document findings and connect them to defensive controls.

DevOps operating habits

Repeatable setup, environment readiness, deployment thinking, monitoring concepts, validation, teardown and clear technical documentation.

Mentoring and preparation

Practical troubleshooting, project feedback, environment preparation and guidance that helps learners explain both what they changed and why.

How I teach

Knowledge transfer with an operating mindset

  • Build confidence through environments learners can operate themselves.
  • Teach attack and defense together in authorized security work.
  • Connect university exercises to production expectations and business constraints.
  • Leave students with repeatable methods, not only a finished demonstration.

Applied case study

Cybersecurity and CTF laboratories

Two isolated environments connect AWS/Linux investigation and Flask/PostgreSQL application security with validation, reset and defensive reasoning.
