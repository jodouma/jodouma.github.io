Selected Work

Work in context, with maturity stated clearly

A concise view of the problem, my contribution, why it matters, and where each initiative stands today.

01

Private Prototype

Arise

The problem or idea

Complex repositories are difficult to understand quickly, especially when context, rules, and validation knowledge are distributed across many files and past sessions.

My contribution

I am exploring a structured, AI-assisted workflow for repository understanding, navigation, analysis, task execution, validation, and durable context. The recovered prototype is currently workflow infrastructure, not a packaged search or retrieval product.

Why it matters

The goal is to help engineers make safer changes with better context. Product capabilities, authorship provenance, and any future public release remain under review; it is not presented as a shipped product or solely authored work.

02

Applied Teaching Lab

Applied CTF and cybersecurity labs

The problem or idea

Learners needed practical environments where they could investigate weaknesses safely and connect each finding to a defensive control.

My contribution

I designed and delivered two isolated labs: one centered on infrastructure and Linux investigation, and another on application and data-access security. I created the learning progression, instructor checks, repeatable setup, validation, reset, teardown, and ethical-use boundaries.

Why it matters

Students practiced observation, documentation, access-control reasoning, and mitigation in controlled environments. Public material excludes credentials, exact solutions, unsafe templates, real targets, and all student information.

03

Early-Stage Concept

MindTrackHealth

The problem or idea

Mental overload can be difficult to recognize early, particularly when work and study patterns become demanding.

My contribution

I am contributing to an early-stage AI concept exploring how non-diagnostic signals might support reflection and earlier awareness. The work is still in exploration and validation.

Why it matters

The concept is not a medical product and is not presented as diagnostic, therapeutic, clinically accurate, regulated, or commercially successful.

04

Teaching Initiative

The problem or idea

Why it matters

The work develops technical confidence while also creating relationships across students, researchers, professors, engineers, and university leadership.
