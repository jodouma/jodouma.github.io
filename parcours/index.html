Professional parcours

From building software to owning reliability

My career grew layer by layer—from understanding business software, to automating cloud delivery, to becoming accountable for the complete production system and teaching the decisions behind it.

I am Youssef Douma, a Senior Platform, Cloud and Reliability Engineer from Tunisia. My journey did not begin with infrastructure alone. It began with an interest in understanding how technology, people and businesses work together.

Start the progressionExplore the proof

Who is Youssef Douma?

Technology, people and business belong to the same system

Studying Computer Science and Business Management, then Data Science and Big Data, taught me to look at a system from several perspectives—how it is built, operated, experienced by users and connected to the wider business.

Over time, my work evolved from application development into cloud architecture, delivery automation, security, incidents, performance, cost and complete production ownership.

Understand the whole system, take responsibility for it, and leave it stronger than you found it.

05

July 2022–July 2026

Four years of production ownership · Apprentus

Becoming accountable for the complete production environment

I joined without a usable predecessor handover or authoritative infrastructure map. Reconstructing the live estate led into security ownership, incident command, observability, full-path performance, data operations, Cloudflare, crawler trust, communications infrastructure, cost and provider decisions. Across four years, the role grew from DevOps execution into functional platform and infrastructure leadership for an international estate serving 80M+ monthly requests across 12 domains.

The Apprentus ownership arc

Twelve acts, one progressively expanding system

The work began with discovery and moved through security, incidents, reliability, scale, platform capabilities, business decisions and knowledge transfer.

  1. 01

    Inherit and map the estate

    Reconstructed providers, servers, services, accounts, dependencies, delivery and recovery paths from live behavior while already operating production.

    Turned inherited uncertainty into an operating model, inventories and runbooks.Read the related engineering story →
  2. 02

    Establish the security baseline

    Distinguished required public services from private dependencies, standardized exposure controls, reviewed authentication activity and strengthened access practices.

    Made security a permanent operating discipline rather than a one-time hardening task.Read the related engineering story →
  3. 03

    Contain the 02:00 CI compromise

    Questioned an anomalously timed session, traced a long-lived Jenkins/CI pivot, contained active movement, eradicated artifacts, recovered service and hardened both environments.

    Closed a compromised access path that had persisted for roughly one year.Read the related engineering story →
  4. 04

    Make production observable and recoverable

    Connected application, host, database, security, certificate, crawler and communications signals to context, safe actions, bounded recovery and permanent fixes.

    Replaced isolated monitoring and repeated out-of-hours recovery with an operating feedback loop built from 300+ hours of direct intervention.Read the related engineering story →
  5. 05

    Scale and tune the request path

    Aligned Cloudflare, Nginx, Passenger, Rails, queues, memory, database connections, Redis and background work instead of maximizing each subsystem independently.

    Built continuous capacity engineering around 80M+ requests across 12 domains.Read the related engineering story →
  6. 06

    Strengthen PostgreSQL and Redis operations

    Managed storage, connections, backups, restores, replication and cache recovery as interdependent production concerns.

    Reduced PostgreSQL from 199 GiB to 133 GiB, reclaiming 66 GiB—about 33%.Read the related engineering story →
  7. 07

    Migrate the 12-domain edge

    Sequenced DNS, Cloudflare proxying, TLS, DNS-01 automation, origin protection, crawler checks, monitoring and rollback one domain at a time.

    Created one observable edge and certificate operating model across 12 markets.Read the related engineering story →
  8. 08

    Automate threat reputation

    Combined suspicious signals, trusted exclusions and reputation context with reversible controls, failure handling, audit state, expiry, recheck and reset.

    Replaced repetitive IP-by-IP response with a reviewable continuous control loop.Read the related engineering story →
  9. 09

    Build crawler trust and visibility control

    Correlated asserted identity, DNS and provider context to protect legitimate Google, Bing, OpenAI, Anthropic and other agents from impersonation and overblocking.

    Connected search visibility, infrastructure pressure and security policy across 12 domains.Read the related engineering story →
  10. 10

    Modernize Jitsi and Jibri

    Integrated JWT access, multidomain aliases, host Nginx and TLS, private service paths, worker orchestration, recording exports and idempotent automation.

    Progressed from a validated five-worker baseline to 100 validated workers; up to 300 remains the future target.Read the related engineering story →
  11. 11

    Optimize cost and represent the platform

    Evaluated waste, overprovisioning, capacity and provider architecture; compared options and represented real technical requirements with Google and other vendors.

    Connected reliability and cost decisions without publishing unsupported savings.Read the related engineering story →
  12. 12

    Make knowledge transferable

    Converted hard-won operating knowledge into documentation, onboarding, progressive responsibilities, mentoring, intern supervision and reviewed change.

    Grew individual production ownership into a repeatable team capability.Read the related engineering story →
